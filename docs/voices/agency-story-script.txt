01-open
Welcome to the session. Think of your business as a song that is already playing. You wrote it. Our job is the mix. Seven short tracks, and by the end you will hear how the parts fit together. Press play.

02-day
Track two. Listen to an ordinary day. A customer sends a message. A post goes out. Someone books. A follow-up comes due. Four different instruments, and right now each one is playing from a different room.

03-gaps
Track three, and this is the boss level. Look at the gaps between the clips. Every gap is a moment where you stop what you are doing and carry something across by hand. Nobody sends an invoice for that time, but you pay it every day.

04-work
Track four. Watch the cables go in. A form on the website fills in the CRM. The CRM starts the follow-up. A post brings an inquiry that lands in the same place. Same instruments, now in one room, playing in time.

05-people
Track five. Machines keep the beat. People make the calls. The repeat work runs by itself, and a real person at KRIV watches the levels. When something needs judgement, a human answers. That is the rule on this desk.

06-owner
Track six, the one most people forget to ask about. Who keeps the master recording? You do. Your domain, your accounts, your customer list. If we ever stop working together, the song leaves with you.

07-next
Final track. You may have noticed there was no price anywhere in this session. That is on purpose. We listen to how your business actually runs first, and only then say what is worth building. Ready for a first conversation?
