01-smile
Hello, and welcome to the Smile Kingdom. This is the story of one tooth, told in eight short parts. Follow it through one ordinary day, and by the end you will see why a check-up matters. Scroll when you are ready.

02-tooth
Meet the hero: a fortress with walls of enamel, the hardest tissue in your whole body. It needs to be. It bites, chews and grinds all day long, and once enamel is lost, the body does not grow it back.

03-meal
Then comes the feast. Breakfast, lunch, a snack in between, and every meal leaves crumbs behind. That part is normal. The catch is who else is eating: the bacteria that live in every mouth love sugars and starches as much as you do.

04-plaque
Here comes the quiet part. Those bacteria form a soft, sticky film called plaque, creeping over the walls like moss. You can brush plaque away. Leave it, and it hardens into tartar, and tartar does not come off with a toothbrush at home.

05-spot
This is the twist. The bacteria in plaque make acids, and acids can wear a tiny weak spot into the enamel, like a first crack in the wall. Early on it usually does not hurt, so you would not know it is there.

06-checkup
Enter the guardian: your dentist. With a mirror, good light and training, a dentist can spot small changes before you feel them, when they are usually simpler to deal with. A professional cleaning also removes the tartar your brush cannot.

07-home
Now your part, and it is a small one. Two minutes of brushing, twice a day, morning and night, with fluoride toothpaste, plus cleaning between the teeth. That daily habit keeps plaque from settling in between visits.

08-again
And that is how the kingdom stays bright. The tooth does its work, you look after it, and a dentist checks what you cannot see. Looked after like this, a tooth can keep doing its job for decades. Your next step: book a check-up with the clinic.
