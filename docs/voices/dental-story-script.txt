01-smile
Hello, and welcome. Before anything about the clinic, here is a short story in eight parts. We are going to follow one tooth through one ordinary day. Stay to the end and you will see why a check-up matters. Scroll when you are ready.

02-tooth
Meet the hero. The shiny outer layer is called enamel, and it is the hardest tissue in your whole body. It needs to be. This one tooth bites, chews and grinds all day long, and once enamel is lost, the body does not grow it back.

03-meal
Breakfast, lunch, a snack in between. Every time you eat, tiny bits stay behind. That part is normal. The catch is who else is eating. The bacteria that live in every mouth like sugars and starches as much as you do.

04-plaque
Here comes the quiet part. Those bacteria form a soft, sticky film called plaque. You can brush plaque away. Leave it, and it hardens into tartar, and tartar does not come off with a toothbrush at home.

05-spot
This is the twist in the story. The bacteria in plaque make acids, and acids can wear a tiny weak spot into the enamel. The tricky part is that early on it usually does not hurt, so you would not know it is there.

06-checkup
Enter the dentist. With a mirror, good light and training, a dentist can spot small changes before you feel them, when they are usually simpler to deal with. A professional cleaning also removes the tartar your brush cannot.

07-home
Now your part, and it is a small one. Two minutes of brushing, twice a day, with fluoride toothpaste, plus cleaning between the teeth. That daily habit is what keeps plaque from settling in between visits.

08-again
And that is the loop. The tooth does its work, you look after it, and a dentist checks what you cannot see. Looked after like this, a tooth can keep doing its job for decades. Next, step inside the clinic and see how it all connects.
