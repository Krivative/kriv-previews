01-start
How a KRIV plan works.
Scroll down for a short walk through what you try, what you pay and what you decide. No pitch, only how it works.

02-trial
Days one to fourteen: you try it first.
The first fourteen days are free. There is nothing to pay and no setup fee, and KRIV staff run the service for you. On day fourteen you keep going or you stop.

03-services
What you can take.
Five monthly services: a website and content creation at three thousand pesos each, an AI live seller at ten thousand pesos, a CRM and system with automations at thirteen thousand pesos, and an AI agent at two thousand five hundred pesos. Take one, or several.

04-paying
Two ways to pay.
Monthly: pay each month and stop at any month. Annual: pay for twelve months at once and KRIV adds free months on top. The service is the same either way.

05-annual
What annual changes.
Take the website. Month by month, fifteen months cost forty-five thousand pesos. On the annual plan you pay thirty-six thousand pesos for the same fifteen months. That is nine thousand pesos less.

06-dates
The dates that matter.
Decide on days fifteen to seventeen for three free months, days eighteen to twenty for two, or days twenty-one to twenty-three for one. From day twenty-four the plan is monthly.

07-bundles
Taking more than one.
Several services on the annual plan lower the yearly price: ten percent for two, up to twenty-five percent for five. The whole saving stops at forty percent.

08-monthly
When monthly is the better choice.
Choose monthly if you are not sure you will keep the service for a year, if paying a year at once would strain this month, or while you are still testing which service helps most.

09-yours
What stays yours.
Your domain, pages, accounts, customer information and content stay yours. Extra work is priced first and starts only after you approve it. KRIV does not promise results or search rankings.

10-decide
Three decisions.
By day fourteen: keep the service, or stop. By day seventeen: monthly or annual, if you want the most free months. Any time: whether to add a service. Build your own plan below.
