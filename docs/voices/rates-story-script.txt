01-start
Welcome. This is a ten-step walkthrough, and none of it is a pitch. Think of it as the rulebook before you play: what you can try, what it costs, and the three choices that are yours to make.

02-trial
Level one is free. For fourteen days you get the real service, run by real KRIV staff, with nothing to pay and no setup fee. Your only job is to watch how it works for your business. On day fourteen you choose: go annual, or walk away.

03-services
Here is the menu. Five services, each one a different tool. A website and content creation, at three thousand pesos a month each. An AI live seller at ten thousand. A CRM with automations at thirteen thousand. An AI agent at two thousand five hundred. Start with one. Add more when you are ready.

04-paying
One way to pay, and it is simple. After the trial there is one plan: annual. You pay for twelve months at once, and there is no month to month plan. Decide early in your trial, and KRIV adds free months on top.

05-annual
Let us do the maths with the website. At the regular rate, fifteen months are worth forty-five thousand pesos. Go annual in the first five days, and those same fifteen months cost thirty-six thousand. Same site, same team. Nine thousand pesos stays with you.

06-dates
This is the timer. The earlier in your trial you choose annual, the bigger the bonus. Days one to five: three free months. Days six to ten: two. Days eleven to fourteen: one. After day fourteen you can still go annual, just without the bonus months.

07-bundles
Now the combo. Stack services on the annual plan and the yearly price drops again. Ten percent off for two. Fifteen for three. Twenty for four. Twenty-five for five. There is a ceiling: your total saving never goes past forty percent.

08-choosing
One service, or a stack. Both are good calls. One service keeps the year simple. A stack lowers the yearly price with the bundle discount. Not sure yet? Use the trial to test, and stop on day fourteen if it is not for you.

09-yours
The fine print, read out loud. Your domain, your pages, your accounts and your customer information are yours, always. Extra work gets a price first, and waits for your yes. And we do not promise results or search rankings.

10-decide
That is the whole game. Three moves, all yours. In the first five days: go annual, for the biggest bonus. By day fourteen: annual, or stop. And at any time: whether to stack another service. Scroll down and build your own plan.
