01-start
Welcome to the Merchant's Road. Ten short stops, and none of it is a pitch. By the end you will know what you can try, what it costs, and the three choices that are yours. KRIV built this system. Let us walk it together.

02-trial
First stop: the lantern path. Fourteen lanterns, one for every free day. Real KRIV staff run the service for your business, with nothing to pay and no setup fee. When the last lantern is lit, on day fourteen, you choose: go annual, or walk away.

03-services
The bazaar has five stalls, each with one yearly price. A website and content creation, at thirty-six thousand pesos a year each. An AI live seller at one hundred eighty thousand, or try it first with a three-month starter at thirty-five thousand. A CRM with automations at one hundred fifty-six thousand. An AI agent at thirty thousand. Start at one stall, and visit more when you are ready.

04-paying
After the trial, the road meets one door, and it opens yearly. You pay for twelve months at once, and there is no month to month plan. Decide early, while the lanterns are still lit, and KRIV adds free months on top.

05-annual
Inside the vault: fifteen chests. Twelve you pay for, and three glow as a gift. Take the website. At the regular rate, fifteen months are worth forty-five thousand pesos. Go annual in the first five days, and you pay thirty-six thousand. Nine thousand pesos stays with you.

06-dates
This is the hourglass, and the portal is slowly closing. Decide on days one to five: three free months. Days six to ten: two. Days eleven to fourteen: one. After day fourteen the door still opens, just without the gift.

07-bundles
At the forge, gems stack into one brighter crystal. Stack services on the annual plan and the yearly price drops: ten percent off for two, fifteen for three, twenty for four, twenty-five for five. Your total saving stops at forty percent.

08-choosing
The crossroads. One path keeps the year simple. The braided road stacks services for the bundle discount. Both lead somewhere good. Not sure yet? Walk the lantern path first, and stop on day fourteen if it is not for you.

09-yours
Every traveller keeps their own keep. Your domain, your pages, your accounts and your customer information are yours, always. Extra work gets a price first, and waits for your yes. And we do not promise results or search rankings.

10-decide
The summit, and three beacons. Light the first by day five, if you want the biggest gift. The second by day fourteen: annual, or stop. The third, any time: whether to stack another service. Scroll down and build your own plan.
