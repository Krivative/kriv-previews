01-start
Welcome. This is a ten-step walkthrough, and none of it is a pitch. Think of it as the rulebook before you play: what you can try, what it costs, and the three choices that are yours to make.

02-trial
Level one is free. For fourteen days you get the real service, run by real KRIV staff, with nothing to pay and no setup fee. Your only job is to watch how it works for your business. On day fourteen you choose: continue, or walk away.

03-services
Here is the menu. Five services, each one a different tool. A website and content creation, at three thousand pesos a month each. An AI live seller at ten thousand. A CRM with automations at thirteen thousand. An AI agent at two thousand five hundred. Start with one. Add more when you are ready.

04-paying
Two ways to pay, and neither one is a trick. Monthly keeps you flexible: pay as you go, and stop whenever you like. Annual is the commitment route: pay for twelve months once, and if you choose it during your trial, KRIV adds free months on top.

05-annual
Let us do the maths with the website. Fifteen months, paid month by month, comes to forty-five thousand pesos. Choose annual in the first five days, and those same fifteen months cost thirty-six thousand. Same site, same team. Nine thousand pesos stays with you.

06-dates
This is the timer. The earlier in your trial you choose annual, the bigger the bonus. Days one to five: three free months. Days six to ten: two. Days eleven to fourteen: one. After day fourteen you can still go annual, just without the bonus months.

07-bundles
Now the combo. Stack services on the annual plan and the yearly price drops again. Ten percent off for two. Fifteen for three. Twenty for four. Twenty-five for five. There is a ceiling: your total saving never goes past forty percent.

08-monthly
A fair rulebook also tells you when not to take the big offer. Go monthly if you are not sure you will keep the service for a full year, if paying a year up front would squeeze this month, or if you are still testing which service helps you most.

09-yours
The fine print, read out loud. Your domain, your pages, your accounts and your customer information are yours, always. Extra work gets a price first, and waits for your yes. And we do not promise results or search rankings.

10-decide
That is the whole game. Three moves, all yours. In the first five days: monthly or annual, if you want the biggest bonus. By day fourteen: keep going, or stop. And at any time: whether to add a service. Scroll down and build your own plan.
