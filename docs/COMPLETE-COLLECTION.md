## What changed

The original Linen/Canvas, Ledger/Index and Torque/Stage remain intact. The other
23 now own separate homepage structures, rather than selecting the same hero and
section sequence with different colors. Examples include Table's facing book
leaves, Gloss's portrait and oversized overlapping typography, Workshop's ruled
photographic plate, Mane's right navigation rail, Shore's rising caption card and
Pressure's perforated message ticket. Existing fonts, palettes and template IDs
remain, with updated fingerprints describing their new structural identities.

Each composition owns its hero, navigation treatment, content order and offering
arrangement. Shared content components render supplied facts and safe links.
Native service disclosures support touch, keyboard and JavaScript-disabled use;
story and question disclosures reveal supplied information only. Mobile layouts
recompose vertically. Motion is limited to short control feedback and disclosure
entrances, with a reduced-motion alternative. No scroll handler or animation/UI
dependency was added. Next.js, React, CSS Modules and the standalone Desk remain.

Every default composition works across industries: contentNiche supplies the
business's terminology separately from the design ID. Existing records are not
bulk migrated. Explicit saved design.sections overrides retain their established
renderer. The three original comparison samples retain the same fictional
business and credited architectural image; the other 23 fictional records and
their reviewed, credited photographs are unchanged. A single photo is used as a
scene rather than repeated to manufacture a gallery. A real multi-photo record
can still use the existing accessible GalleryViewer.

The source gallery now has an actual screenshot on every design card. Lead Desk's
existing picker reads all 26 composition labels and signatures automatically.
The four new presentation files participate in visual-version hashing: changing
the code invalidates stale visual approval while keeping decision history.
Refresh verification captures before visually reviewing an affected concept.
Publication and outreach still require their separate founder actions.
